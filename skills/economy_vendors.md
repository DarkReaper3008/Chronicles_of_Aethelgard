# Economy, Crafting & Vendor Guidelines
- **NPC Smiths & Brewery Master:** Create dedicated vendor interfaces for Weapon Smiths (upgrade attack power), Armor Smiths (upgrade defense rank), and Brewery Masters (craft healing potions/buff tonics).
- **Coin & Artifact Store:** Implement a trading post where players can exchange collected gold coins for rare artifacts or spell books.
- **Inventory & Economy Loop:** Tie coin collection seamlessly to enemy drops, chest looting, and open-world exploration.
